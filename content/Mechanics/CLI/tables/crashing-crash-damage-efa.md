# Crashing; Crash Damage
*Source: Eberron: Forge of the Artificer p. 104* 

**Crash Damage**

| Size of Creature or Object Struck | Bludgeoning Damage |
|-----------------------------------|--------------------|
| Medium or smaller | `2d10` |
| Large | `4d10` |
| Huge | `8d10` |
| Gargantuan | `16d10` |
