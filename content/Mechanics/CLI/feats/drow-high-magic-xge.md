# Drow High Magic 


***Prerequisites*** [Elf](Mechanics/CLI/races/elf-xphb.md)

You learn more of the magic typical of dark elves. You learn the [detect magic](Mechanics/CLI/spells/detect-magic-xphb.md) spell and can cast it at will, without expending a spell slot. You also learn [levitate](Mechanics/CLI/spells/levitate-xphb.md) and [dispel magic](Mechanics/CLI/spells/dispel-magic-xphb.md), each of which you can cast once without expending a spell slot. You regain the ability to cast those two spells in this way when you finish a long rest. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for all three spells.
