# Hex: Misfortune
*cantrip, Divination*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 round

Choose one creature you can see within range. Until the spell ends at the end of your next turn, whenever the target rolls a 20 on the `d20` of a [D20 Test](Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/d20-test-xphb.md), the roll instead becomes a 1.

**Classes**: [Witch](Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-witch.md)

*Source: Valda's Spire of Secrets (2024) p. 0*
