# Hex: Pox
*cantrip, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 10 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 round

One creature you can see within range makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the target has the [Poisoned](Mechanics/CLI/rules/conditions.md#Poisoned) condition until the spell ends at the end of your next turn.

**Classes**: [Cleric (Death Domain)](Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-death-domain-dmg.md "subclass=DMG;class=XPHB"); [Witch](Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-witch.md)
