# Helm of Telepathy
*Wondrous item, major, uncommon (requires attunement)*  
![](Mechanics/CLI/items/img/helm-of-telepathy.webp#right)


While wearing this helm, you can use an action to cast the [detect thoughts](Mechanics/CLI/spells/detect-thoughts-xphb.md) spell (save DC 13) from it. As long as you maintain [concentration](Mechanics/CLI/rules/conditions.md#Concentration) on the spell, you can use a bonus action to send a telepathic message to a creature you are focused on. It can reply—using a bonus action to do so—while your focus on it continues.

While focusing on a creature with [detect thoughts](Mechanics/CLI/spells/detect-thoughts-xphb.md), you can use an action to cast the [suggestion](Mechanics/CLI/spells/suggestion-xphb.md) spell (save DC 13) from the helm on that creature. Once used, the suggestion property can't be used again until the next dawn.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 174. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>*
