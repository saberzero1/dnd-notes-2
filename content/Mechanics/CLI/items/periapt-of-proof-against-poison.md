# Periapt of Proof against Poison
*Wondrous item, major, rare*  


- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

This delicate silver chain has a brilliant-cut black gem pendant. While you wear it, poisons have no effect on you. You are immune to the [poisoned](Mechanics/CLI/rules/conditions.md#Poisoned) condition and have immunity to poison damage.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 184. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>*
